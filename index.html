Found a bug or want something added?

Email me. A few things make it easy for me to act on:

  • Bug or wrong tone: say what you expected and what you got (too bright, too much gain, wrong pickup).
  • Missing gear or song: give the brand and exact model, or the song, artist and album.
  • Your setup: your song, guitar, amp and pedals are filled in for you below.
  • Room matters: mention bedroom or gig volume if it affects the sound.
  • Keep it short: one topic per email, and no passwords or personal details.
